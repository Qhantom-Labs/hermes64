# polar_pod pack notes (3.1.0)

A retro-futurist polar habitat matched to the private reference study in STYLE-BIBLE-v0.md. The
references are read-only study material and are never copied, traced or named.

- **Palette:** 32 colours. Navy to cobalt to periwinkle to lilac to pearl to white ramps, four chrome
  steps, wool/knit greys, ice blues, and sparing warm accents (`ember`, `glow_*`) for lamps and
  screens.
- **Materials:**
  - Glossy molded shells: `body` uses a gloss texture over the pearl ramp.
  - Chrome via env map (`env_chrome`, tinted `chrome_3`). Glass via `env_glass`.
  - Quilted vinyl: `quilt` (wave channels) and `cushion` (diamond). Plus `knit` and `fur`.
  - Snow and ice terrain.
  - The view card shows a starry night with lit domes.
- **Hero presets** (each is a look-critique subject; see `reports/LOOK-CRITIQUE.md`):
  - `furn.pod`: default bowl, bowl_portholes, chaise, chaise_visor, egg, conversation_pit.
  - `furn.bench`: default curled chrome blob bench on a plinth; chrome_legs.
  - `furn.console`: default mushroom console with a screen ring; desk_triple_crt; terminal.
  - `furn.table`: disc_orb with a dark sphere; flower_table.
  - `arch.window`: molded oval, rounded_molded, panorama, porthole (glass off, so the view card shows).
  - `arch.door`: hatch with a porthole leaf.
  - `vehicle.craft`: saucer with two black window bands, a light ring and a dome; snow_sled.
- **Shells and buildings:** round dome room shell (oval_lounge variant) and a round dome house.
- **Fixtures:** 35 layout slots. For example, seat_lounge is the chaise, seat_pit is the
  conversation_pit, table_low is disc_orb, console is desk_triple_crt, and window_main is
  rounded_molded.
- **Cast:** references only, supplied by lane-characters: faceless expedition figures and habitat cats.

**Known limits** (honest):
- The 400-triangle budget for large furniture forces coarse shells on the most detailed pods.
- The chaise still reads slightly like a boat.
- The preview glass is opaque, so polar windows ship with glass off.
