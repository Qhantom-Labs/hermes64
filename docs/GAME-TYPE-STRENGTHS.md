# Hermes64 game types: what is strong, rough and not supported

Measured snapshots from development, not a certification. "Strong" names a measured capability, not a rating of
looks or fun. Story proofs, bot verdicts and the measured style audit are separate results; `SOLID` and `PLAYABLE`
are tool verdicts, not an aesthetic endorsement. Numbers come from the development record of each feature (one machine,
an Apple M4 Pro Mac, unless stated). Supported mechanics: [AUTHORING-SCOPE.md](AUTHORING-SCOPE.md).

| Type | Strong (measured) | Rough (measured limits) | Not supported or not established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exploration / story | A directly authored tower game: 4 rooms, 2 NPC dialogues, key and clue gates; proved, physical bot path complete, replay and save passed, headless boot of the exported macOS app passed. | That game's style audit was FAIL 0/100; a western branching example FAIL 42/100, although both endings completed. Starter rooms were a 4 x 4 grid in that snapshot (clear squares 1.77 to 3.52 m, hand-authored median 6.52 m). Current create-flow room scaling is documented in `skills/hermes64-create/SKILL.md`; these old measurements do not describe every current layout. | A finite room graph and authored dialogue, not a seamless world or live AI characters. |
| Grid puzzle | Puzzle Hall: 4 puzzles; shortest solutions 18, 13, 13 and 30 steps; 155,637 grid states explored; the physical bot reached the ending with 0 state mismatches; 4/4 known-bad controls rejected; replay of 2,270 frames / 38 hashes matched. | One winning route plus probes, not every physical ordering. The agent create flow does not make puzzles by itself; an agent added one by hand in an earlier test. | A separate puzzle authoring workflow; no general physics puzzles. |
| Arena combat and boss | 5 moves landed and 4 challenges cleared, including a 3-boss finale; a deliberate death added 600 ticks, retry 60 ticks; 2 strict replays equal. ## Geometry and frame time

Default loaded-room cap 6,000 triangles; declared arena cap 8,000. A 7,684-triangle rooftop scene measured wall-frame
p50 1.36 ms and p95 6.6 ms, with 21 of 600 frames over 8 ms and p99 37 ms. That is not a web measurement and not a
guarantee for other characters or machines. The robot kit's 7,996-triangle / 10-robot capacity is arithmetic from
measured part costs, not an exercised ten-robot fight; the robot proof frame had 4,762 triangles (hero and nine robots).
